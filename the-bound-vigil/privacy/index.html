Privacy Policy

The Bound Vigil, by Krowsingre Publishing LLC · Effective 29 October 2026

The Bound Vigil ("TBV") is an ebook and audiobook reader for Windows, iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: Krowsingre Publishing LLC does not collect, see, store or sell any of your data. TBV has no account with us, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your devices

Your library lives on your own devices. That includes your books, audiobooks, notes, highlights, reading places, ratings, reading history, collections, settings and anything you write in Scribe. None of it is sent to us.

Sync uses your own cloud

If you turn on sync, TBV copies your library between your devices through a cloud service you choose and sign in to yourself: iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, MEGA, a WebDAV server, a synced folder, or your own Supabase project. TBV only uses its own folder in that service. Your data is then covered by that service's own privacy policy. We never have access to it.

Outside services TBV talks to

Some features connect to other services, only when you use them:

  • Book details and covers: when you look up a book's details or series, TBV sends that book's title, author or ISBN to services such as Open Library, Google Books, ISFDB, Wikidata and Audible.
  • Suggested reading: TBV reads Krowsingre Publishing's list of suggested books from this website once a day. It also sends the names of up to three authors you read most to Open Library, to find their other books. You can turn this off in Preferences › Appearance › Sanctum.
  • Hardcover: if you connect your Hardcover account, TBV sends your ratings and reading status there.
  • Audiobookshelf: if you connect your own Audiobookshelf server, TBV signs in to it and keeps your listening place in step.
  • Catalogues and Discover: free book catalogues (such as Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks) and any websites you visit in TBV's built-in browser see your visit, as they would in any browser. Browsing history and site data stay on your device unless you choose to sync them. You can clear them at any time.
  • Read-along: TBV downloads its speech model once from Hugging Face. Lining up a book with its audiobook happens on your device, and the audio is never uploaded.
  • Sending to nearby devices: Grimoire sends books directly over your local network, never through the internet.
  • Updates: on Windows, TBV checks GitHub for new versions.
  • Purchases: buying TBV is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store or the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Permissions

On iPhone and iPad, TBV may ask for access to your photos (only to pick a profile picture or a collection's picture) and to files you choose to import. It uses your local network only to find your own devices for Grimoire.

Deleting your data

Because everything is yours and stays with you, you're in control. You can remove any book or entry inside TBV, clear your reading history in Preferences, or delete TBV's folder from your cloud service. Uninstalling TBV removes its data from that device.

Children

TBV is not aimed at children under 13, and we collect no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
