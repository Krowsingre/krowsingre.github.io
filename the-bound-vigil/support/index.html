Support

The Bound Vigil, by Krowsingre Publishing LLC

Need a hand with The Bound Vigil ("TBV")? Email krowsingre@gmail.com and say which device you use (Windows PC, iPhone or iPad) and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Plans and purchases

  • Yearly: $14.99 a year. On iPhone and iPad the first month is free. Cancel any time in your App Store or Microsoft account; you keep it until the year ends.
  • Lifetime: $44.99 once, yours for good.
  • On a PC, TBV can be tried free for 30 days first.
  • Each store sells its own plans, so a purchase on iPhone covers your iPhone and iPad, and a purchase in the Microsoft Store covers your Windows PCs.
  • New phone or reinstalled? In Preferences, find Your copy and press I bought it already.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple, or your Microsoft account's order history.

Sync

  • TBV syncs through the cloud you choose in Preferences › Sync. Use the same service and the same library on every device.
  • The sync circle in the top corner shows whether you're up to date. Tap it to sync now.
  • On iPhone, changes from the computer arrive when you open TBV, or when iOS lets TBV check in the background.

Adding books

  • On a PC, drag books onto TBV or use Import in the sync circle's menu.
  • On iPhone and iPad, use Import, share a book to TBV from another app, or find free books in Discover › Catalogues.
  • TBV never changes your book files. Edits to a book's details are kept separately.

Privacy

TBV doesn't collect your data. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
